Our Origin Story

Saffron India began as a micro-estate near the Jhelum river where our great-grandmother Noor Jahan pioneered shade drying to retain crocin levels. Today, every blossom is still hand-plucked and graded by the same family.

5th Generation Farm ISO 3632 Grade I Traceable Supply Chain

Our terroir features mineral-rich soil, glacial meltwater, and a dramatic day-night temperature swing that deepens flavor complexity. We cultivate limited batches to maintain potency and ensure fair wages for artisan pickers.

Manifesto

Craftsmanship + Conscious Luxury

Slow Harvest

Flowers bloom for just three weeks annually; we harvest before sunrise to capture floral volatiles.

Glass + Gold Packaging

UV-protective violet glass jars with micro-lot numbering for complete traceability.

Giving Back
